# Robustness

Robust optimization distinguishes uncertain quantities from decision variables and seeks decisions that remain feasible or effective over an uncertainty set.

A generic robust constraint has the form

```math
g(x,u)\le0,\qquad\forall u\in\mathcal U,
```

where $u$ denotes uncertain parameters and $\mathcal U$ is the uncertainty set.

This layer will be developed only after the deterministic baseline models are validated.
