# Economic Objectives

A generic lifecycle cost may be decomposed as

```math
C(x)=C_{\mathrm{cap}}(x)+C_{\mathrm{op}}(x)+C_{\mathrm{maint}}(x).
```

When each component is linear in the decision vector, the economic model can remain within linear programming.

Every cost term should specify its accounting boundary, time horizon, and units.
