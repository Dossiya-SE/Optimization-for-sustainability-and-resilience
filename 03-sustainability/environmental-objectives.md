# Environmental Objectives

A generic linear environmental objective can be written as

```math
E(x)=\sum_{i\in\mathcal I}e_i x_i,
```

where $e_i$ is an environmental burden per unit activity.

Examples include greenhouse-gas emissions, embodied energy, water withdrawals, waste, and land use.

The coefficient units must be stated so that the units of $E(x)$ are interpretable.
