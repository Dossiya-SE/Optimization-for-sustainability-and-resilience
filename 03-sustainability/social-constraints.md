# Social and Service Constraints

Social performance should be represented through explicit measurable quantities when possible.

A generic minimum-service requirement is

```math
\operatorname{service}_g(x)\ge \underline{s}_g,\qquad\forall g\in\mathcal G,
```

where $g$ indexes population groups, geographic areas, or service classes.

A rigorous formulation must define the service metric, units, threshold, and affected group.
